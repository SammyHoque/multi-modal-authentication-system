How to Register

Select your desired authentication method(s) from the four options below.

Then press Enable Webcam and record your password. Each method has 3 seconds for recording.

The recording duration and completion status will be displayed below the camera screen.

Once all selected methods have been recorded successfully, press Save.

Detailed guidance for each selected method will appear below the options before the camera section.

Method Guidance

Gesture

Make a sequence of hand gestures within the 3 seconds of recording. It is recommended to display your hand clearly in front of the webcam before starting.

Facial Motion

Make a sequence of facial gestures such as opening your mouth, tilting your head, or winking within 3 seconds of recording. It is recommended to show your face clearly.

Voice

After selecting Voice, a text box will appear. Type your desired phrase or word. Then, when recording starts, say the exact phrase within the 3 seconds of recording. It is recommended to remember the tone of the recording.

Face ID

When recording, make sure your face is clear in the webcam and wait until the recording completes.


You will need to say this exact phrase when logging in.
